Activities

Work, organising and debate, from high school to now. Most of it is debate. It is listed here because it is a large part of what I did, not because all of it leads to law.

Work

    LVT Education Joint Stock Company

    Legal and HR Officer

    I drafted and reviewed labour contracts, internal policies and company documents against Vietnamese labour law, kept personnel records and handled onboarding. When something in an employment practice looked risky, I raised it with management.

Leading and organising

  • Aug 2019 to May 2023

    Maple Debate, Le Hong Phong High School debate club

    President

    I wrote a training programme that took two intakes, 40 members in total, from novice to advanced, and coached 21 newcomers myself; the CV records 36 notable results from that period. I set up partnerships with Fulbright University and with debate clubs at Gia Dinh High School, THPT THSP and Hue National School, and co-chaired three workshops and The Amazing LHP Race, which drew more than 350 people.

    AMPLE Project, a non-profit debate initiative

    Founder and event organiser

    We ran a two-day offline debate competition for nearly 150 participants, working with specialists from the Fulbright University Debate Club. Total revenue was about VND 68 million, and we received VND 35 million in sponsorship. I also coordinated a five-day debate camp for nearly 100 participants, sponsored by Vietnam Airlines, Crimson and Nova College.
