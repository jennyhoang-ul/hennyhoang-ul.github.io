Proof in administrative cases

My first project as principal investigator: a comparative study of proof in administrative litigation in selected countries, and its reference value for Vietnam.

The question

In an administrative case, a person challenges a decision of the state, and the state usually holds most of the relevant documents. How the task of proving is shared between the parties, and how far the court gathers evidence itself, shapes whether that person can win at all. The project compared how selected countries handle this.

My role

I led the student team as principal investigator. It was my first research project in that role.

Method

Comparative legal analysis of the rules on proof in administrative litigation in selected countries, read against Vietnamese law.

Result

  • Consolation Prize, Faculty-level Student Research Award.
  • Graded Excellent at defence.

Related work

It was the first of my four student research projects. The next three were on platform work, electronic data messages and personal data in electronic evidence.
