Selected Academic Projects

  • CISG in Vietnamese Courts: Applicability, Recognition and the Risk of Homeward Interpretation

    Project Coordinator · Group 3, Contracts for the International Sale of Goods · legal research, applied analysis, AI-assisted web tool | Sep to Oct 2026

    • Coordinated a team study of how Vietnamese courts identify and apply the CISG, structuring it into four linked parts: data, case analysis, conditional risk forecast and recommendations.
    • Designed a step-by-step screening framework that tests each judgment against CISG applicability rules (Arts. 1, 2, 3, 6, 10, 100) and Vietnamese conflict-of-laws rules before recording how the court treated the Convention.
    • In the team’s sample of 197 judicial documents, 27 were assessed as CISG-relevant and 2 showed substantive application; argued that this evidences a recognition gap, not proof of “homeward” interpretation, and reframed the forecast accordingly.
    • Specified and tested an AI-assisted Python/Streamlit tool for PDF intake, guided screening, record review, statistics and a practice mode; debugged it through GitHub.

  • Juvenile Prisoners’ Family Contact in Vietnam: National Law against UN Standards

    Project Coordinator · Group 7, Juvenile Justice · domestic, international and foreign law research | Oct 2026

    • Coordinated a group study of juvenile prisoners’ rights to visits and communication under the Law on Juvenile Justice 2024 and the Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments 2025.
    • Co-drafted the introduction and legal framework, separating binding CRC obligations from interpretive guidance (General Comments 24, 25) and non-binding UN rules (Havana, Beijing, Mandela).
    • Directed a seven-criterion comparison (including digital communication and monitoring, disciplinary restrictions and remedies) and set out two competing readings of how the specialised and general statutes interact.
    • Reviewed the group’s comparison with Berlin, England and Wales, Queensland and California and its eight reform proposals (138-page group draft).
