Ride-hailing drivers and their contracts

Team research on how selected countries protect drivers who work through ride-hailing platforms under contract, and what their approaches offer Vietnam. The paper was published in the conference proceedings.

The question

Drivers on ride-hailing platforms work under contracts written by the platform, and whether they are treated as employees, partners or something in between decides which protections they get. The project looked at how selected countries answer that question and what reference value their answers have for Vietnam.

My role

I was a member of the research team and a co-author of the paper.

Method

Comparative legal analysis of how selected countries regulate the contractual relationship between drivers and ride-hailing companies, read against Vietnamese law.

Result and publication

  • Second Prize, 2024 Young Scientists Conference.
  • Published in the conference proceedings, "Vietnam in the Context of Integration and Globalization".

It is listed with my other outputs on the writing page.
