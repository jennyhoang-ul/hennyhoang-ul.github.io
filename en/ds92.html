Student research, 2024 to 2025

Collecting electronic data messages

A faculty research project I led on the legal measures used to collect electronic data messages, such as emails and chat messages, in selected countries, and what Vietnam could take from them.

Programme
Student Scientific Research, Ho Chi Minh City University of Law, project DS92
Period
2024 to 2025
My role
Principal investigator
Method
Comparative legal analysis
Result
Graded Good at defence, October 2025

The question

Vietnamese procedural law accepts electronic data as a source of evidence, but collecting it raises questions that paper evidence does not: who may collect it, by which measure, and how its integrity is kept. The project compared the measures selected countries use for this and asked what Vietnam could adopt.

My role

I led the student team as principal investigator, from the research plan to the defence.

Method

Comparative legal analysis of the collection measures in selected countries, read against Vietnamese law, with recommendations drawn from the comparison.

Result

  • Graded Good at defence (Certificate 922/GCN-NCKHSV, October 2025).

Related work

The following year I joined project HS61, on protecting personal data when electronic evidence is collected.
