Student research, 2025 to 2026

Personal data in the collection of electronic evidence

A faculty research project on the safeguards that should protect personal data when investigators collect electronic evidence in criminal cases, comparing the criminal procedure laws of selected countries with Vietnam's.

Programme
Student Scientific Research, Ho Chi Minh City University of Law, project HS61
Period
2025 to 2026
My role
Team member
Method
Comparative legal analysis
Result
Second Prize, faculty level; graded Excellent at defence

The question

Electronic evidence rarely holds only what an investigation needs. A phone or an online account carries messages, photos and contacts, many of them about people who have nothing to do with the case. The project asked what limits and safeguards criminal procedure law places on collecting that material, and what Vietnam could take from the rules of selected countries.

My role

I was a member of the research team; another student led the project as principal investigator.

Method

Comparative legal analysis: the criminal procedure rules of selected countries were read against Vietnamese law, and lessons for Vietnam were drawn from the comparison. It used the same comparative frame as my other student research projects.

Result

  • Second Prize, Faculty-level Student Research Award (Decision 1044/QĐ-ĐHL, October 2025).
  • Graded Excellent at defence (Certificate 435/GCN-NCKHSV).

Related work

The year before, I led project DS92 on collecting electronic data messages. Personal data is also the subject of my current research notes on children's data.
