I am in my fourth year of law and business. Most of my research takes a legal problem in Vietnam1 and looks at how other systems handle it2. Lately those problems have been about data, evidence and technology3.

  1. From proof in administrative cases (2023) to whether courts apply the CISG (2026). Eleven projects are listed in the archive.
  2. Selected foreign laws, UN standards and, in one project, 197 Vietnamese judgments read against the rules that should have applied.
  3. Two projects on personal data and electronic evidence. COPPA, the GDPR and AI law are what I am reading now; I do not claim expertise in them yet.

Selected work

Five pieces chosen to show different sides of the work: two course projects in depth, then three student research projects.

01

Course project, September to October 2026

CISG in Vietnamese courts: when the law applies but the judgment does not mention it

In our team's sample of 197 judicial documents, 27 had a basis for applying the CISG and 2 actually applied it. The case study explains why that gap, on its own, does not prove courts prefer domestic law.

Field
International sale of goods, treaty applicability
My role
Project coordinator (self-reported)
Method
Applicability screening, case coding, case analysis
Output
Slide deck, analysis drafts, screening tool

02

Course project, October 2026

Juvenile prisoners' family contact: a higher ceiling is not a guaranteed floor

A seven-criterion comparison of the 2024 juvenile justice law and the 2025 sentence-execution law with UN standards and four foreign approaches, including how monitoring of phone and video contact should be limited.

Field
Juvenile justice, children's rights
My role
Project coordinator (self-reported); introduction and legal framework
Method
Doctrinal and comparative analysis
Output
138-page group draft, eight reform proposals

    Student research, project HS61 | 2025 to 2026

    Personal data in the collection of electronic evidence

    A comparison of the safeguards for personal data that apply when authorities collect electronic evidence in criminal cases, in selected countries and in Vietnam.

    Second Prize, faculty level (Decision 1044/QĐ-ĐHL); graded Excellent (Certificate 435/GCN-NCKHSV)

  2. 04

    Student research, project DS92 | 2024 to 2025

    Collecting electronic data messages

    What legal measures selected countries use to collect electronic data messages, and what Vietnam could take from them.

    Graded Good (Certificate 922/GCN-NCKHSV, October 2025)

  3. 05

    Student research, conference paper | 2024

    Rights of ride-hailing drivers in their contracts

    How selected countries protect drivers who work through ride-hailing platforms, and what that suggests for Vietnam.

    Second Prize, 2024 Young Scientists Conference; published in the proceedings
