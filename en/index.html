Research

Six projects from 2023 to 2026, newest first. Each one opens into a full write-up.

  • 07

    Course project | 2026

    CISG in Vietnamese courts

    We screened 197 judicial documents for whether the CISG should have applied, then checked what the courts actually wrote. 27 had a basis for it; 2 applied it.

    Slide deck, analysis drafts, screening tool

  • 09

    Course project | 2026

    Juvenile prisoners' family contact

    How the 2024 juvenile justice law and the 2025 sentence-execution law protect contact with family, compared on seven criteria with UN standards and four foreign approaches.

    138-page group draft, eight reform proposals

  • 06

    Student research | 2025 to 2026

    Personal data in the collection of electronic evidence

    Which safeguards for personal data apply when authorities collect electronic evidence in criminal cases, in selected countries and in Vietnam.

    Second Prize, faculty level; graded Excellent

  • 05

    Student research | 2024 to 2025

    Collecting electronic data messages

    What legal measures selected countries use to collect electronic data messages, and what Vietnam could take from them.

    Graded Good, October 2025

  • 04

    Student research, published paper | 2024

    Ride-hailing drivers and their contracts

    How selected countries protect drivers who work through ride-hailing platforms, and what that suggests for Vietnam.

    Second Prize, 2024 Young Scientists Conference; published in the proceedings

  • 03

    Student research | 2023 to 2024

    Proof in administrative cases

    My first project as principal investigator: how selected countries handle proof in administrative litigation, and what Vietnam could draw from them.

    Consolation Prize, faculty level; graded Excellent

What I work on

The areas I have researched, the habits I bring to each project, and what I am reading now.

Areas

  • International sale of goods (CISG)
  • Juvenile justice and children's rights
  • Electronic evidence and personal data
  • Platform work
  • Administrative litigation

Habits

  • Read the statute before the commentary
  • Compare systems on the same fixed questions
  • Code judgments and count with clear denominators
  • Set two readings of a rule side by side
  • Specify small research tools, built with AI help

Reading now

  • Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law 2025
  • The GDPR on children and health data
