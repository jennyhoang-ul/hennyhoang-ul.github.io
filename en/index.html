Work out when a rule applies

I break a legal rule into its conditions, exceptions and the facts that decide each one. In the CISG project that became a screening sequence across Articles 1, 2, 3, 6, 10 and 100 and the conflict-of-laws rules, with an explicit "needs human review" branch where facts were missing.

Evidence: CISG, method

Compare legal systems on fixed criteria

I compare sources question by question and keep their legal force apart: a treaty, a committee's interpretation and a non-binding UN standard are not the same thing. The juvenile justice project used seven fixed criteria across Vietnamese, UN and four foreign sources.

Evidence: JCJ, comparative method

Keep conclusions within the evidence

When our CISG data could not show "homeward" interpretation, I changed the project's claim instead of stretching the numbers. I separate what a source says, what we infer, and what is still a risk.

Evidence: CISG, turning point

Coordinate a research team

I have run two student research projects as principal investigator and coordinated two 2026 course projects: setting questions, dividing work, defining quality checks and integrating drafts.
