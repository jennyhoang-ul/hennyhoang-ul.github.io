Research notes

Questions I am working through. Nothing here is a finished output or legal advice.

When do children's data rules apply to a wellbeing app?

Three regimes may matter for an app offered worldwide and free to students in Vietnam:

RegimeWhat I need to check first
COPPA Rule (United States), amended 2025, compliance date 22 April 2026Is the service directed to children under 13, or does the operator know it collects their data?
Personal Data Protection Law No. 91/2025/QH15 (Vietnam), in force since 1 January 2026How children's data and sensitive data are handled, and what a transfer abroad requires.
GDPR (EU)Whether the service targets or monitors people in the EU, and the age of consent in each member state.

The plan is to compare them on fixed questions, as in the juvenile justice project: age threshold, legal basis, parental consent, sensitive data, transfers abroad, deletion and retention.

In progress Reading the primary texts now. No conclusions yet.

The CISG in courts and in arbitration

A follow-up to the course project: do arbitral tribunals in Vietnam recognise and interpret the CISG differently from courts? I am working on an outline for a journal article, separate from the group's work.

In progress Outline stage, November 2026.

When does a streak become a dark pattern?

Wellbeing apps use streaks and rewards to build habits, and regulators in the United States and the EU have called some design practices manipulative. Where the line sits is partly a legal question and partly a question for behavioural science, so it is one I would want to work on with people from that field.

In progress Open question.
