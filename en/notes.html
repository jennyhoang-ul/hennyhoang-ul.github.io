Questions I am working through. None of these is a finished output or legal advice.

When do children's data rules apply to a wellbeing app?

Three regimes may matter for an app offered worldwide and free to Vietnamese students:

RegimeStarting condition I need to check
COPPA Rule (United States), amended 2025, compliance date 22 April 2026Is the service directed to children under 13, or does the operator know it collects their data?
Personal Data Protection Law No. 91/2025/QH15 (Vietnam), in force 1 January 2026How are children's data and sensitive data handled, and what is required to transfer data abroad?
GDPR (EU)Does the service target or monitor people in the EU? What age of consent applies in each member state?

My plan is to compare them on fixed criteria, as in the juvenile justice project: age threshold, legal basis, parental consent, sensitive data, cross-border transfer, deletion and retention.

In development Reading the primary texts now. No conclusions yet.

A follow-up to the course project: do arbitral tribunals in Vietnam recognise and interpret the CISG differently from courts? I am refining an outline for a journal article, separate from the group's work.

In development Outline stage, November 2026.

When does a streak become a dark pattern?

Wellbeing apps use streaks and rewards to build habits. Regulators in the United States and the EU have described some design practices as manipulative. Where is the line, and who should decide it: lawyers, behavioural scientists or both? This is a question I would want to work on with people who know behavioural science, not alone.
