  3. 2026

    Course project, Group 3

    CISG in Vietnamese courts

    We screened 197 published judgments for whether the CISG should have applied, then checked what the courts actually wrote. 27 had a basis for it; 2 applied it.

    Role
    Project coordinator (self-reported)
    Method
    Applicability screening, case coding, case analysis
    Output
    Slide deck, analysis drafts, screening tool

    Documented

  4. 2026

    Course project, Group 7

    Juvenile prisoners' family contact

    How the 2024 juvenile justice law and the 2025 sentence-execution law protect contact with family, compared on seven criteria with UN standards and four foreign approaches.

    Role
    Project coordinator (self-reported); introduction and legal framework
    Method
    Doctrinal and comparative analysis
    Output
    138-page group draft, eight reform proposals

    Documented

    Role
    Specified, tested and debugged, with AI writing much of the code
    Method
    Requirements, testing, GitHub
    Output
    Source code (October 2026 version); live link not yet checked

    Documented
